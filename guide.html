How to use Aether Tiles

Features marked 1.1 arrived with that update. Menu names match the app in English.

Aether Tiles replaces your Android home screen with Metro-style live tiles: each tile opens an app and also shows something useful (time, notifications, battery, your photos, news…). This guide covers the basics in a few minutes.

1. Make it your home screen

  • When you open the app, accept setting it as your default home app. If it doesn't ask, go to Aether Tiles settings → Default home app.
  • To go back to your usual home screen: Aether Tiles settings → Go back to your previous launcher, and pick the one you had.
  • The phone's Home button always takes you back to the top of your tiles and closes any open folder.

2. Getting around

  • All apps: swipe left on the home screen (or tap the Apps tile). At the bottom you'll find the magnifier to search, the house to go back and the gear for Aether Tiles settings.
  • Pin an app to the home screen: in the app list, tap the pin next to it.
  • Landscape 1.1: tiles are laid out in blocks side by side and you scroll sideways. There, apps open from the Apps tile.

3. Moving and organising tiles

  • Move: long-press a tile or widget and, without lifting your finger, drag it. When you let go, the others make room and no gaps are left.
  • Customize: long-press a tile and release without moving it (or long-press an empty spot). The tile list opens. Tap a tile to:
    • move it with the ← ↑ ↓ → arrows,
    • change its size (1x1, 2x1, 2x2 or 4x2),
    • Configure its look and content (see below),
    • remove it with the red button.
  • Folders: in Customize, tap an app and choose Group in folder. Inside a folder you can Add app and Rename.
  • Add: at the bottom there's ＋ Apps and ＋ Widget (your apps' widgets); in the list, the system tiles you removed and the option to add news or a YouTube channel.
  • Finish with Done to save or Discard to leave everything as it was. Reset restores the original layout.

4. Configuring a tile

Customize → tap the tile → Configure. Depending on the tile you'll see:

  • Size and Material: Glass (translucent), Solid (solid colour, like the originals) or Clear (border only).
  • Tile colour and background: you can use one of your own photos.
  • Clock: choose the dial (digital, analogue, sport, chronograph or a photo of yours).
  • Gallery and Camera: Choose folder so the tile shows the latest photos from that folder. Android only gives the app access to that folder.
  • Music: under On tap, open pick your player, or leave Automatic (the last app you listened with).
  • News: the tile's source and Update now.
  • Also: Information density, the tile's Privacy mode and Animation style. Tap Apply to save.

5. Live tiles

  • Notifications and conversations on tiles: in Aether Tiles settings → Live tiles → Notifications, messages and music, grant notification access. Your app tiles then show counters and flip through recent notifications 1.1, and the Music tile shows what's playing with its buttons.
  • Calendar: grant permission from Live tiles to see your next event.
  • Battery, Settings, Phone, Camera and Apps 1.1 show live data: charge, temperature and time left; storage and memory; carrier and signal; last photo; recent apps.
  • News and YouTube 1.1: in Customize, add news or a YouTube channel and paste a website or channel address. The tile cycles through headlines; tap it to open the one showing.
  • All of this is processed on your phone. More in the privacy policy.

6. Appearance

In Aether Tiles settings → Appearance:

  • Wallpaper: the system one, a photo of yours (enables the glass blur) or a solid colour.
  • Dim wallpaper and Glass opacity: raise the opacity if text is hard to read on light wallpapers.
  • Compact grid 1.1: tiles closer together and a bit larger.
  • Thin font 1.1: lighter text, like classic tiles.
  • LED light on the edges: its colour and speed.

7. Privacy and motion

  • Privacy: hides messages, events and what's playing, keeping only the counters. Handy when showing your phone to someone.
  • Motion: turns off non-essential animations.

8. Backup

  • Save backup creates a file with your layout, folders and appearance.
  • Restore backup brings it back, on this phone or another. Widgets belong to each phone, so add them again if you switch phones.
