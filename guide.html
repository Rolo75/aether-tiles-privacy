How to use Aether Tiles

Version 1.1 · Features marked 1.1 arrive with that update. The app is in Spanish for now; the Spanish menu names are shown in grey.

Aether Tiles replaces your Android home screen with Metro-style live tiles: each tile opens an app and also shows something useful (time, notifications, battery, your photos, news…). This guide covers the basics in a few minutes.

1. Make it your home screen

  • When you open the app, accept setting it as your default home app. If it doesn't ask, go to Aether settings → Default home apps (Aplicaciones de inicio predeterminadas).
  • To go back to your old launcher: Aether settings → Back to previous launcher (Volver al launcher anterior) and pick it.
  • The phone's Home button always takes you back to the top of your tiles and closes any open folder.

2. Getting around

  • All apps: swipe left on the home screen (or tap the Apps tile). At the bottom you'll find search, home, and the gear for Aether settings.
  • Pin an app to the home screen: in the app list, tap the pin next to it.
  • Landscape 1.1: tiles are laid out in blocks side by side and you scroll sideways. Open the app list with the Apps tile.

3. Moving and organising tiles

  • Move: long-press a tile or widget and, without lifting your finger, drag it. When you let go, the others make room and no gaps are left.
  • Customise (Personalizar): long-press a tile and release without moving it (or long-press an empty spot). Tap a tile in the list to:
    • move it with the ← ↑ ↓ → arrows,
    • Resize it (Cambiar tamaño): 1x1, 2x1, 2x2 or 4x2,
    • Configure its look and content (Configurar), see below,
    • remove it with the red button.
  • Folders: in Customise, tap an app and choose Group into folder (Agrupar en carpeta). Folders can be renamed and you can add more apps.
  • Add: at the bottom there's ＋ Apps and ＋ Widget; in the list you can bring back system tiles you removed and add news or a YouTube channel (＋ Noticias o canal de YouTube).
  • Finish with Done (Hecho) to save or Discard (Descartar). Reset (Restablecer) restores the original layout.

4. Configuring a tile

Customise → tap the tile → Configure. Depending on the tile you'll see:

  • Size and Material: Glass (translucent), Metro (solid colour, like the originals) or Transparent (border only).
  • Tile colour and background: you can use one of your own photos.
  • Clock: choose the face (Esfera): digital, analogue, sport, chronograph or your own photo.
  • Gallery and Camera: Choose folder (Elegir carpeta) so the tile shows the latest photos from it. Android only gives the app access to that folder.
  • Music: under Al tocar, abrir pick your player, or leave Automatic (the last app you listened with).
  • News: the tile's source and Update now.
  • Also: information density, per-tile privacy and animation style. Tap Apply (Aplicar) to save.

5. Live tiles

  • Notifications on tiles: in Aether settings → Live tiles → Notifications, messages and music (Mosaicos vivos → Avisos, mensajes y música), grant notification access. App tiles then show counters and flip through recent notifications 1.1, and the Music tile shows what's playing with controls.
  • Calendar: grant permission from the same section to see your next event.
  • Battery, Settings, Phone, Camera and Apps 1.1 show live data: charge, temperature and time left; storage and memory; carrier and signal; last photo; recent apps.
  • News and YouTube 1.1: in Customise, add news or a YouTube channel and paste a website or channel address. The tile cycles through headlines; tap it to open the one showing.
  • All of this is processed on your phone. More in the privacy policy.

6. Appearance

In Aether settings → Appearance (Apariencia):

  • Wallpaper: the system one, a photo of yours (enables the glass blur) or a solid colour.
  • Dim wallpaper and glass opacity: raise the opacity if text is hard to read on light wallpapers.
  • Compact grid 1.1 (Cuadrícula compacta): tiles closer together and a bit larger.
  • Light font 1.1 (Letra fina): lighter text, like classic tiles.
  • LED light on the edges: its colour and speed.

7. Privacy and motion

  • Privacy (Privacidad): hides messages, events and what's playing, keeping only the counters. Handy when showing your phone to someone.
  • Motion (Movimiento): turns off non-essential animations.

8. Backup

  • Save backup (Guardar copia) creates a file with your layout, folders and appearance.
  • Restore backup (Restaurar copia) brings it back, on this phone or another. Widgets belong to each phone, so add them again if you switch phones.
