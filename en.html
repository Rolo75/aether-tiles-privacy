Aether Tiles privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · This is a translation; if in doubt, the Spanish version prevails.

Aether Tiles is a launcher (home screen) for Android. This policy explains what data the app uses and what it does with it. The short version: everything stays on your phone.

What data it uses and why

  • Installed apps. The list of apps is read to show them in the app drawer, to let you pin them as tiles and to offer your music and video players in the Music tile.
  • Notifications (optional). If you grant notification access, the app reads the title and text of visible notifications (for chats, their latest messages) to show counters and recent notifications on the tiles. It also reads active media sessions (title, artist, cover or thumbnail, progress) for the Music tile and its play, pause and skip buttons. In the Aether notification panel you can open, dismiss or snooze notifications and turn on Do Not Disturb; this only happens when you ask for it. The list of apps you mute in Aether is stored in the app.
  • Photo folder (optional). If you choose a folder for the Gallery tile, Android gives the app access to that folder only. The app reads the most recent photos to show them on the tile. It doesn't copy or send them anywhere, and you can remove the folder at any time from Configure.
  • News and channels (optional). If you add a news or YouTube channel tile, the app downloads that site's public feed (headlines, links and thumbnails) directly from it and stores it in the app. Only the address you chose is requested; none of your data is sent and no accounts are used.
  • Calendar (optional). If you grant calendar permission, the app checks, read-only, the next event in the following 48 hours to show it on the Calendar tile.
  • Photos you choose. If you pick a photo as wallpaper or as a tile background, Android's photo picker is used: the app only receives that photo, never access to your gallery. A cropped copy is kept inside the app.
  • Last played and music history. For the Music tile and hub, the app stores the title, artist, a small cover and the app name of the last 12 songs or videos played. Older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Weather (optional). If you add the Weather tile, the city name you type is sent to Open-Meteo to look it up, and then its coordinates to download the forecast. Your location is not used. The city and the latest forecast are stored in the app. Weather data: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).
  • Favourite people (optional). If you choose favourites with Android's contact picker, only their name and number are stored in the app, with no access to the rest of your contacts. If you pin a person to the home screen, their number becomes part of the tile layout.
  • Lend mode (optional). The PIN is not stored as such, only an encrypted fingerprint of it. While the phone is lent, and only then, the app uses the "Usage access" you grant in Android settings to know which app is open and to return to the lent screen if it isn't an allowed one. That information is neither stored nor sent.
  • Notes (optional). What you write in the Notes tile is stored only in the app and is not sent anywhere.
  • Preferences. Your tile layout and settings (appearance, privacy, motion) are stored in the app's internal storage.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't send your data off the device. The app has no servers. It only connects to the internet to download the public feeds of the news tiles you add and, if you use the Weather tile, the Open-Meteo forecast for the city you choose. As with any website visit, that service sees your connection's IP address.
  • It doesn't store the content of your notifications, messages or events: it is only kept in memory while it's shown.
  • It includes no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on, the system may include the Aether Tiles tile layout and settings in your Google account backup. Photos and last played items are left out of that backup. That backup is managed by Google according to your settings.

How to remove permissions and delete data

You can remove notification access in Android Settings › Notifications › Notification access, "Usage access" in Settings › Apps › Special access, and the calendar permission in Settings › Apps › Aether Tiles › Permissions. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Settings › Apps › Aether Tiles › Storage, deletes everything it stores on the device. (Menu names vary slightly between phone brands.)

Children

Aether Tiles is not specifically aimed at children and does not collect data from any user.
